A few good questions

Before we get started.

What kind of projects do you take on?

We work on product packaging, Canva design, 2D graphic design, and responsive websites. Share what you have in mind and we can discuss whether it fits.

How does a project usually begin?

Send a short project inquiry with your goals, service needs, and any timing or budget context. We’ll follow up to clarify scope and discuss next steps.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. We can discuss a combined scope—for example, an identity and packaging system, or brand design alongside a website.

Do you work with businesses at different stages?

We’re happy to hear from businesses at any stage. The right scope depends on your goals and what you need support with now.
